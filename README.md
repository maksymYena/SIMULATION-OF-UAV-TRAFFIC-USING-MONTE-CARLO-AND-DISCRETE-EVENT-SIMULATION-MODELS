# **SIMULATION OF UAV TRAFFIC IN URBAN AERIAL NETWORKS USING MONTE-CARLO AND DISCRETE-EVENT SIMULATION MODELS**

## **Subject Matter**  
Integrated simulation model of UAV traffic management, which combines Monte Carlo methods and discrete-event modeling to assess the effectiveness of airspace allocation, taking into account unpredictable changes in the urban environment.

---

## **Goal**  
To develop, implement, and evaluate a unique simulation model for UAV traffic management with adaptive capabilities.

---

## **Article**

https://dndivsovt.com/index.php/ts/article/view/542

---

## **Tasks**  
- Develop a new multi-layer model of spatial constraints for urban airspace.  
- Create an innovative adaptive redirection algorithm based on real data (distance, speed, route), taking into account random influences.  
- Perform a simulation analysis of the effectiveness of UAV flow optimization using Monte Carlo and DPM methods for different scenarios.

---

## **Methods**  
- Integration of Monte Carlo and DPM to create a simulation.  
- Adaptive algorithms for real-time UAV route redirection.

---

## **Results**  
Integration of redirection algorithms and adaptive simulation methods is a promising direction for improving the efficiency and safety of urban mobility. The use of a combination of algorithm and simulation can:  
- Significantly reduce the number of accidents.  
- Optimize traffic flows.  
- Contribute to the creation of sustainable transport systems in cities.

---

## **Conclusions**  
The proposed algorithm and resulting simulation for urban mobility management demonstrated the ability to:  
- Improve traffic flows.  
- Reduce the risk of collisions.  
- Increase overall airspace safety.  

The experimental results confirmed the effectiveness of redirection algorithms and simulation methods.

---

`UAV traffic simulation`, `urban air mobility`, `Monte Carlo method`, `discrete-event modeling`, `airspace management`, `flow analysis`, `dynamic control`.
